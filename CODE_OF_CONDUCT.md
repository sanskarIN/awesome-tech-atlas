# Community Code of Conduct

Awesome Tech Atlas welcomes learners and maintainers from different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Ask clarifying questions in good faith, credit other contributors, and share enough context for others to reproduce documentation problems. Make space for new contributors and differing viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, unwanted personal attention, personal attacks, threatening behavior, and publishing another person's private information are not permitted. Spam, deceptive submissions, and abusive responses to review are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct reports, contact the maintainer **privately** through the contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/sanskarIN); please **do not** share sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Include relevant links and facts without unnecessary personal information.

Maintainers may request edits, remove material, close discussions, restrict participation, or block repeat offenders. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Good-faith reports will not be penalized.

This code applies to issues, PRs, reviews, and other spaces run for this repository.
