## Why it matters

Practical guidance for starting, contributing to, and maintaining an open-source community.

## Good fit

Designing contribution workflows, community norms, and responsible project stewardship.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Covers contributor onboarding and maintainer concerns rather than only code.
- **Limitation:** General advice must be adapted to a project's size, governance, and capacity.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.
