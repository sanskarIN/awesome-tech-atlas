## Why it matters

Official Wireshark documentation and learning materials for inspecting packet captures.

## Good fit

Understanding protocol conversations and diagnosing traffic on networks you are authorized to analyze.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Packet inspection helps connect abstract protocol concepts with actual traffic fields.
- **Limitation:** Encrypted traffic and incomplete captures can limit visibility; privacy and network authorization matter.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.

## Try it

Open a sample capture you own or one explicitly provided for training, then inspect a protocol's header fields.
