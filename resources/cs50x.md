## Why it matters

A first course should connect writing a program to explaining its behavior. CS50x gives learners a progression from visual programming to C, then higher-level languages and applications.

## Good fit

Learners starting without a programming background or self-taught developers filling gaps in memory, data structures and problem decomposition.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** The progression and problem sets encourage reasoning about correctness rather than merely copying syntax.
- **Limitation:** The weekly assignments can take significant effort; the introductory survey is not a substitute for dedicated systems or algorithms courses.

These are contextual selection notes, not independently measured performance or reliability claims.

## Try it

Begin with Week 0 and write a small interactive program. Then attempt a C exercise from Week 1 without looking at solutions. Keep a notebook of inputs, expected outputs and one bug you fixed. Before moving on, explain the difference between an algorithm and its implementation.
