## Why it matters

Official documentation for the VS Code editor, extensions, debugging, source control, and settings.

## Good fit

Editing code across multiple languages with integrated tooling.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Extensible debugging, terminal, and source-control workflows in a single editor.
- **Limitation:** Language support quality often depends on extensions; product and open-source builds may have different terms.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.
