## Good fit

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Well-documented declarative APIs and operational concepts support large deployments.
- **Limitation:** Cluster setup, networking, security, and observability add considerable operational complexity.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.
