## Why it matters

Documentation for array computation and transformations such as automatic differentiation and compilation.

## Good fit

Numerical research needing gradients, vectorization, and compiled array operations.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Transformation-oriented APIs can express gradients and batched numerical functions.
- **Limitation:** Functional patterns, accelerator setup, and compilation behavior need learning and profiling.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.
