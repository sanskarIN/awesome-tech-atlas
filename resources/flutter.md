## Why it matters

Official Flutter guides for Dart-based UI, widgets, tooling, and platform integrations.

## Good fit

Developing an app UI for multiple supported platforms from a shared project.

## Strength and trade-off

- **Strength:** Consistent widget-based UI workflows and documented platform integrations.
- **Limitation:** Native features still require platform-specific configuration; app size and platform conventions may matter.

These are contextual selection notes, **not** independent speed, reliability, or security benchmarks.
